THE 1980s ERA

🌾 Labor in the Fields

Around forty years ago, life in Vietnam was filled with hardship. Families lacked food and clothing, and adults worked tirelessly from dawn till dusk under the sun across vast rice paddies to sustain their loved ones.

📚 Walking to School & Helping Out

Children walked long distances on dirt pathways to modest brick and tile classrooms. Despite scarce materials, they studied with devotion and returned home to help their parents with daily chores.

❤️ Enduring Love & Community Support

Though material life was difficult, human bonds remained unbreakable. Families and neighbors shared whatever little they had, constantly loving, caring for, and supporting each other through every challenge.
